Santa Clara licensed-capacity gap

Synthetic fixture data for CI. These are not real Santa Clara figures.

Each hexagon shows licensed child care seats per child aged 0 to 5 after every seat is shared among all cells that can reach it within a 15-minute drive (two-step floating catchment, 2SFCA).

  • under 0.10 seats per child (least access)
  • 0.10 to 0.25
  • 0.25 to 0.50
  • 0.50 or more
  • not enough data (abstained)
  • child care center (family child care homes are never shown)

Click a cell to see its numbers and the cells it shares seats with.

Licensed seats only. These figures count licensed child care centers and family child care homes. Informal family, friend and neighbor (FFN) care, which California exempts from licensing, is not counted, so a licensed-capacity gap compares licensed supply with children; it does not show whether families have care. Family child care homes are placed at ZIP centroids to protect home addresses.
